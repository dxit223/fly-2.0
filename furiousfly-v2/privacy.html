Privacy Policy

1. Data Security Commitment

At Furious Fly (operating on `furiousfly.in`), our highest technical directive is to respect and secure the integrity of user information. Under the leadership of founder Hitesh Kumar, we build secure systems that encrypt all client inquiries and database logs end-to-end. We do not sell or leak user datasets.

2. Collection of Information

We collect information that you explicitly submit to us via our project inquiry forms (such as Name, Email Address, Telephone Numbers, and custom project scopes). We utilize standard sanitizers to prevent cross-site scripts and SQL injection parameters.

3. Sovereign AI Datasets

Our custom AI division specializes in private enterprise sandboxing. Any data submitted for our customized machine learning training is processed via isolated pipelines. We do not feed private business intelligence into public general LLM interfaces.

4. Browser Cookies and Storage

We utilize temporary client-side local storage and lightweight functional cookies to persist contact receipts and blog preferences. You may clear your browser cookies and local storage parameters at any time to remove these transient records immediately.

5. Legal Inquiries

If you have any questions or require data deletion logs, reach out directly to Hitesh Kumar via info@furiousfly.in.
